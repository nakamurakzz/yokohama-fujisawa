Nogeyama Zoo closes from 7 January: Yokohama's three zoos, priced

For families choosing between free Nogeyama, Zoorasia and Kanazawa Zoo: entry, parking, the hours each one takes, and what is left of Nogeyama's calendar before it shuts for renewal works.

Yokohama runs three zoos. Nogeyama Zoo (野毛山動物園) is free, small and a 15-minute walk from Sakuragicho Station (桜木町), and it closes for renewal works from 7 January 2027. The city plans to reopen it in 2029 and has not fixed a date.

Zoorasia (よこはま動物園ズーラシア) is the big one: ¥800 for an adult, and 3 to 4 hours for one lap. Kanazawa Zoo (金沢動物園) is ¥500 and sits on a hill inside a nature park.

So this winter the choice has a deadline. If you want the free zoo, you have until the first days of January. If you have a car and a whole day, go to Zoorasia. If you want half a day with a long slide next door, go to Kanazawa.

With my daughters, the zoo I go to most is Zoorasia. It is 40 minutes from our home. Nogeyama is awkward to reach by car, so we rarely go. Kanazawa is where I sometimes go for a barbecue, and my impression is that it is hillier than the other two.

Everything else here comes from the zoos’ and the city’s own pages, as of 25 November 2026.

The three zoos side by side

The city owns all three and one foundation, Yokohama-shi Midori no Kyokai (横浜市緑の協会), runs them. Their standard hours match: 9:30 to 16:30, with last entry at 16:00. All three are shut from 29 December to 1 January.

Each lap time excludes breaks and meals. When the closed day falls on a public holiday, the zoo opens that day and closes the next. Kanazawa has no closed days in May and October.

Zoorasia covers 13.7 times the ground Nogeyama does. That one number explains most of the table.

The city’s FAQ counts three zoos and one branch, a 0.2-hectare children’s zoo at Makigahara (万騎が原ちびっこ動物園) in Asahi ward, also free. It was not researched for this piece beyond that line.

Nogeyama: free, and shut from 7 January 2027

Nogeyama Zoo charges nobody. Its notice, updated on 12 June 2026, says the zoo closes for renewal works from 7 January 2027. Reopening is planned for 2029 and, in the notice’s own words, not decided. The rest of Nogeyama Park stays open.

The notice names the first closed day. It does not name the last open one. The zoo’s pages give this much:

  • Monday 28 December to Friday 1 January: closed for the year end.
  • Saturday 2 and Sunday 3 January: a poster-calendar giveaway at the entrance from 9:30, for the first 200 visitors each day. The zoo calls it the last one before the closure.
  • Monday 4 January: a Monday, the regular closed day.
  • Tuesday 5 and Wednesday 6 January: open days by the normal rule.

No page read for this piece confirms 4, 5 or 6 January, and the zoo’s visitor page says it sometimes opens on a closed day. The safe last days are 2 and 3 January.

There is no car park. The entrance has 3 spaces for disabled visitors, which cannot be reserved. On foot the zoo is 15 minutes from Sakuragicho on JR and the Blue Line, and 10 minutes from Hinodecho (日ノ出町) on the Keikyu Line. The zoo’s driving directions end with the words “the zoo is at the top of the slope”.

City bus route 89 runs every day and stops at Nogeyama Dobutsuen-mae (野毛山動物園前), right by the zoo. The Transportation Bureau’s page puts it at platform 1 of the east exit bus terminal at Yokohama Station and platform 8 at Sakuragicho Station. The same page gives about 20 minutes from Yokohama Station.

The fare is ¥220, or ¥110 for a child. Up to 2 children under 6 ride free with each guardian. The city has applied to raise the adult fare to ¥240 in January 2027, and on its page updated on 23 October 2026 the date was still open.

On the way back, read the sign on the bus. Some buses from the zoo stop go through to Yokohama Station and some end at Sakuragicho.

The petting area, Nakayoshi Hiroba (なかよし広場), has guinea pigs and mice. Every session has to be booked in advance on the zoo’s reservation system. Booking opens at 12:00 one week before the day, first come, first served.

There are 7 sessions of 15 minutes each, 3 in the morning from 10:20 and 4 in the afternoon from 13:30. Each takes 10 groups of up to 5 people, and one person can join one session a day. A child of elementary-school age or younger cannot book alone.

English listings lag behind. Time Out’s page on the zoo is dated 8 January 2020 and says “numbered tickets are required” for the petting area. Good Luck Trip’s family list, updated on 14 May 2026, describes Nogeyama without mentioning the closure.

Zoorasia: the one that takes a day

Zoorasia kept about 95 species and 470 animals at the end of March 2026. Every public-transport route on its access page involves a bus.

Buses marked Yokohama Dobutsuen (よこはま動物園) leave the north exits of Tsurugamine (鶴ヶ峰) and Mitsukyo (三ツ境) on the Sotetsu Line and take about 15 minutes. From the south exit of Nakayama (中山), on the JR Yokohama Line and the Green Line subway, the bus takes about 18 minutes. A bus from Yokohama Station takes about 1 hour, and the zoo’s page warns that there are few of them.

The car park holds 2,200 cars and costs ¥1,000 a day. It opens 1 hour before the zoo and shuts 1 hour after it, and stops taking cars 30 minutes before the zoo closes. On Saturdays, Sundays and holidays in November 2026 the zoo is trying out a ¥1,500 fee. Two of those days are left, 28 and 29 November.

Inside, a bus runs direct between the Zou-san stop (ゾウさんのりば) by the main gate and the north gate stop. It costs ¥200 from junior-high age up and ¥100 for elementary-school children, and it is cash only. The north gate itself opens only on weekends, holidays and busy days.

The ticket machines take credit cards, and the entry gates take a transport IC card such as Suica or PASMO. Keep the ticket: showing it lets you back in on the same day.

Kanazawa Zoo: the one on a hill

Kanazawa Zoo takes up 12.8 hectares of Kanazawa Nature Park, which the zoo’s site puts at about 600,000 square metres of hills. It keeps mostly plant-eaters. The city’s FAQ names Indian elephants, giraffes, Indian rhinoceroses and koalas. The zoo counted 51 species and 1,154 animals on 31 March 2026.

There are two car parks, both ¥600, both normally open 9:00 to 17:00 with last entry at 16:30.

The front car park (正面口駐車場) has 450 spaces and is entered from an ordinary road, the Sasage-Kamariya road (笹下釜利谷道路). From there the Koala Bus (コアラバス), a free shuttle, takes about 5 minutes, and the entrance is about 3 minutes further on foot. Walk the whole way and the zoo’s own figure is about 20 minutes for an adult, uphill.

The expressway-side car park (高速側駐車場) has 750 spaces and is about 3 minutes on foot from the entrance. It connects only to the Yokohama-Yokosuka Road (横浜横須賀道路), through a dedicated gate at the left end of the Kamariya toll plaza (釜利谷料金所). You cannot reach it from ordinary roads.

By train, go to Kanazawa-bunko (金沢文庫) on the Keikyu Line and take the west exit to bus stop 1. The Keikyu bus for Nomura Jutaku Center (野村住宅センター) takes about 10 minutes to Natsuyamazaka-ue (夏山坂上), and the entrance is 6 minutes on foot up a gentle slope. On Saturdays, Sundays and holidays an express bus runs direct to the zoo.

The park’s botanical zone, outside the zoo gate, is open 9:00 to 17:00 every day of the year, and the fee page lists a charge only for the zoo. It has a 100 m roller slide.

The barbecue areas are in the botanical zone too, in Nonbiri Nohara (のんびり野原) and part of Nandaro-zaka (なんだろ坂), and are booked through an outside operator’s website. The zoo’s page gives no prices for them. The park’s own description of Nandaro-zaka is a steep slope.

Zoorasia’s car park is ¥1,500 on 28 and 29 November.

On Saturdays, elementary, junior-high and high-school students enter both paid zoos free. Zoorasia asks for a student ID. Kanazawa accepts anything that shows the child’s age.

Going to Nogeyama by bus from Yokohama Station and back costs the same family ¥1,320 at today’s fares. Walking from Sakuragicho costs nothing.

The annual pass costs ¥2,000, is sold to visitors aged 18 and over, and is valid for one year from the day of purchase at both paid zoos. Three Zoorasia visits at ¥800 come to ¥2,400, so the pass pays for itself on the third. The fee pages list no pass for anyone under 18.

What is not good

Nogeyama is already emptying. The zoo’s relocation list, updated on 19 October 2026, dates the moves of its Japanese black bears, raccoon dogs and Abyssinian colobus monkeys to Zoorasia between 9 March and 9 November 2026. The relocation list has the condor, ostrich and flamingos going to zoos in Tokushima, Hamamatsu and Saitama Prefecture, with dates from 23 March to 27 April 2026.

The relocation list does not say which moves have been carried out. Check it before you promise a child a bear.

The relocation list also has red pandas and Humboldt penguins going to Zoorasia for the closure, with no dates. Giraffes, Grevy’s zebras and chimpanzees are among the animals it shows staying on the site.

Nogeyama has 4 strollers to lend and no car park. The petting area takes 70 groups a day, and without a booking you do not get a session.

Zoorasia is a bus ride from every station, and the ride from Yokohama Station is about an hour. The in-park bus is a second fare on top of entry. One ride for 2 adults and 2 elementary-school children is ¥600 in cash.

At Kanazawa, a missed Koala Bus from the front car park means a 20-minute climb. Both car parks are shut on the zoo’s closed days, even though the botanical zone is open all year. The expressway-side car park is useless if you came by ordinary roads.

All three stop letting people in at 16:00.

Some things were not checked for this piece: bus fares to Zoorasia and Kanazawa, which are set by other bus companies, how fast the petting sessions fill, and the queues at the gates and car parks. There are no numbers for those here.

Which one, and when

Before 7 January, without a car: Nogeyama. Book the guinea pigs at 12:00 exactly one week ahead. Go before 28 December, or on 2 or 3 January.

With a car and a full day: Zoorasia, on a Saturday if your children are at school. The ¥1,500 parking trial covers 28 and 29 November.

With half a day, or a barbecue booked: Kanazawa. Use the front car park and wait for the Koala Bus.

From 7 January two zoos are left, and the cheaper visit for that family is Kanazawa on a Saturday, at ¥1,000.
